Glue module between ``stock_removal_zone_fifo`` and ``product_expiry``,
installed automatically when both are.

It adds a **Zone-Level FEFO** removal strategy, the expiry-aware
counterpart of Zone-Level FIFO. Goods closest to their removal date are
still taken first; the zone entry date only breaks ties between lots
expiring on the same date:

::

   removal_date, zone_in_date, in_date, id

The standard FEFO strategy is left untouched.

Usage
=====

Set ``Zone-Level FEFO`` as the removal strategy of a location, or of a
product category.

The zones and the zone entry date are the ones of
``stock_removal_zone_fifo``, so a location carrying ``Zone-Level FEFO``
is a zone too.
