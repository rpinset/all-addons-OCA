Synchronizes all the Shopify product media with the extra product media
of the Odoo eCommerce (``product.image``).

Without this glue module, Shopify Connector only synchronizes the main
image of each product and the images of its variants, and leaves the
other Shopify images untouched. With it, the other Shopify images are
imported as extra product media, and the extra product media managed in
Odoo are exported to Shopify.

The module installs automatically when Shopify Connector and eCommerce
are both installed.
