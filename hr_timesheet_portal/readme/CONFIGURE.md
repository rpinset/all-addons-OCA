To configure this module, grant users access to the "Editable timesheets" privilege.
You can do this by:

1. Going to Settings > Users & Companies > Users, selecting a user, and
   setting "Editable timesheets" to "User" under the Website section, OR

2. Going to Settings > Users & Companies > Groups, searching for the
   "Editable timesheets" privilege, and adding users directly to its "User" group

If you want all portal users to be able to edit timesheets, add the "User" group of the
"Editable timesheets" privilege as an implied group of the Portal group.
