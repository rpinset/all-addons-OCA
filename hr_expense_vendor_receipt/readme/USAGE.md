To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to Expenses and create a new one that is paid by Employee
1. Create report and Submit to Manager
1. Change Journal to Receipt journal
1. Approve this Expense Sheet
1. Go to the Journal Entry clicking on the Smart Button
1. You can modify the Purchase Receipt like a normal Purchase Receipt (partner, bill ref, journal, etc)
1. Post the Receipt move
1. Reset to Draft the Expense Sheet and see how Receipt is deleted
