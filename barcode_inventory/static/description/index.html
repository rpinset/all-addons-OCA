Barcode Inventory

Perform inventory adjustments and stock counts directly from the Barcode scanner app.

The operator picks an internal location, scans the products found there (with barcode_gs1 installed, a GS1 label also brings its lot/serial and quantity), types the counted quantity, and applies. Each adjustment is recorded as a stock_inventory adjustment group — draft → in progress → done — so every barcode count leaves a traceable, auditable record with its own move lines, using Odoo’s native on-hand adjustment mechanism (stock.quant).

This is a feature module on top of barcode_scanner: it registers its own screens, scan handling and home-screen tile into the scanner core without patching it, so you install only what you need.

Usage

From the Barcode app home screen, tap Inventory Adjustment.

Count a location

  1. Pick or scan the location to count. Scanning a location barcode jumps straight into the count; otherwise choose one from the searchable list of internal locations.
  2. The count screen shows the stock the location holds, one line per product and lot. Count by scanning each product: a plain barcode adds one unit. With barcode_gs1 installed, a GS1 label also brings its lot/serial and its quantity; without it, the scanner reads plain EAN-13 barcodes only. You can also type the counted quantity in each line, decimals included, which then shows how far it is from the stock.
  3. To count something not shown, tap “+” and scan or search the product. A freshly scanned or added product jumps to the top of the list and is highlighted, so you always see what the scan just changed. Only storable products can be counted: services and non-storable consumables are not offered, and scanning one says why it is refused.
  4. Tap Apply Adjustment to record the count. If the stock of a counted product changed while you counted — goods came in or went out — nothing is applied yet: the lines show the stock as it is now, and you choose whether to apply your count anyway or to count again.

Lots and serial numbers

A lot/serial-tracked product needs its lot/serial before it can be applied — read from a GS1 label, or typed into the line (the lots a count at that location can use are suggested; a new name is created on the server). The name is matched exactly first, as lots differing only in case are different lots; in another case it is only taken when it names a single lot. The same product can appear on several lines, one per lot or serial number, so it stays available in the “+” picker even after it already has a line.

A serial number counts one unit: reading it again warns instead of counting it twice.

What it records

Each adjustment is applied as a stock_inventory adjustment group (draft → in progress → done), scoped to exactly the counted products at the counted location, using Odoo’s native on-hand adjustment. Every barcode count therefore leaves one traceable, auditable record with its own move lines. Lots are created in the counted location’s company.

A count covers the stock at the location itself, outside packages and not owned by a partner: its sublocations are counted on their own, and stock in a package or owned by someone else is neither shown nor changed.

As in the back office, applying a count requires Inventory Administrator rights. Other users are told so when they open Inventory Adjustment.
